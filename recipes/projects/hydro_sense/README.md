# HAZA Hydro Sense

Hydro Sense projects are for hydroponics and water-based growing systems. The
series starts with the reservoir readings that make the biggest difference:
water temperature, pH, nutrient strength, and the air conditions around the
grow area.

The point is not to automate dosing on day one. The first useful step is seeing
what the reservoir is doing, how quickly it drifts, and whether the water is in
a sane range before making any control decisions.

The exact hardware, measurements, and variant ladder still need Pascal's brief.
For now, this folder is only the starting scaffold for the series.
